# Plan: refresh the worksheet for Kubernetes v1.37 (Garhwal)

> Working plan for the v1.37 content refresh, executed September 2026.
> Facts come only from kubernetes.io release and feature blogs, the v1.37 CHANGELOG, and the feature-gates reference.
> Third-party summaries were checked and rejected where they disagreed with the CHANGELOG
> (example: kubelet deprecated config flag removal is deferred to v1.38, not done in v1.37).

## Goal

Move the book's baseline from v1.36 to v1.37 (released 2026-08-26) without changing its shape:
no new flows, no new figures, no diagram edits.
Every change is a swap or a clause added at a line where the book already makes the claim.
Length budgets in STYLE.md are nearly exhausted, so each addition pairs with a cut in the same chapter.
